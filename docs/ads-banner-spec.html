A "ratio" means the width × height of a banner in pixels. For example, 970 × 90 is a wide thin banner that sits across the page, while 300 × 250 is a small square that fits in a side column.

Overview — sizes we use

Five banner sizes are used across the site. They follow the standard sizes recognised by the global ad industry.

SizeRatio (pixels)Where it fits
Wide banner 970 × 90 Spans the full width of the page, thin strip
Large banner 970 × 250 Spans the full width, tall — high impact
Sidebar square 300 × 250 Small square that fits in the right-side column
Native (in-grid) — Mixed into the listing grid, looks like a regular card
Compact 728 × 90 Compact horizontal strip (legacy, rarely used)
